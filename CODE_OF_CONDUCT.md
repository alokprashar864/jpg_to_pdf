## 1. Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy technical community.

---

## 2. Our Standards of Conduct

### Positive & Expected Behavior
Examples of conduct that contributes to a positive, collaborative environment include:
- **Demonstrating Empathy & Kindness:** Treating fellow contributors, reviewers, and users with dignity and patience.
- **Respecting Diverse Viewpoints:** Valuing differing opinions, architectural proposals, and varying levels of engineering experience.
- **Constructive Feedback:** Delivering and gracefully accepting well-reasoned, actionable critique on code reviews, RFCs, and documentation.
- **Accountability:** Promptly acknowledging mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from the experience.
- **Community Focus:** Prioritizing what is best for the project's long-term sustainability, security, and users over personal ego.

### Unacceptable & Prohibited Conduct
The following behaviors are strictly prohibited across all project touchpoints:
- **Harassment & Discrimination:** Derogatory, demeaning, or hostile comments related to personal identity, background, or demographic traits.
- **Trolling & Flaming:** Sustained disruption of issues, PRs, or discussions, provocative baiting, insults, or ad hominem attacks.
- **Sexualized Language & Imagery:** Inappropriate sexual language, advances, or sexualized media in issues, code reviews, or profile assets.
- **Doxxing & Privacy Invasions:** Publishing another individual’s private personal details (e.g., physical address, private phone number, personal email) without explicit written consent.
- **Spamming & Commercial Solicitation:** Unsolicited marketing, affiliate links, or deceptive advertising within issues, comments, or pull requests.
- **Bad-Faith Contributions:** Deliberately submitting malicious code payloads, hidden backdoors, or automated PR spam.

## 3. Scope of Applicability

This Code of Conduct applies within all official project spaces, including:
- The GitHub repository (Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, Commits, Wiki).
- Any official chat groups, Discord channels, or communication forums.
- Any setting where an individual is officially representing the project in public (e.g., conferences, hackathons, podcasts, technical blog articles).

## 4. Enforcement Guidelines & Tiered Escalation

Project maintainers follow a clear, four-tier enforcement matrix to evaluate and address violations proportionately:

| Tier | Severity | Action Taken | Consequences |
|:---|:---:|:---|:---|
| **Level 1: Correction** | Minor | Private Written Notice | Maintainer provides private written clarification explaining why the behavior violated our standards. The offending comment/content is redacted or removed. |
| **Level 2: Warning** | Moderate / Repeated | Formal Warning & Moderation | A formal warning is issued with clear expectations of consequences for continued behavior. The contributor may be placed on temporary interaction limits. |
| **Level 3: Temporary Ban** | Serious | Time-Limited Suspension | The contributor is temporarily banned from interacting with the repository (issues, PRs, comments) for a period of **7 to 30 days**. |
| **Level 4: Permanent Ban** | Severe / Malicious | Permanent Expulsion | Permanent ban from all repository touchpoints and community organizations. Reserved for hate speech, severe harassment, malicious code insertion, or chronic abuse. |

## 5. Reporting Procedures & Confidentiality

If you experience, witness, or are alerted to unacceptable behavior:

1. **Submit a Report:** Contact the leadership team directly via email at `conduct@alokprashar.com` (or reach out to the project administrator via private GitHub profile contact).
2. **Include Documentation:**
   - URL links to the affected Issue, PR, or Discussion.
   - Screenshots of the offending remarks or interactions.
   - Context surrounding the incident and usernames involved.
3. **Confidentiality Guarantee:** All reports will be handled with strict confidentiality. The identity of the reporter will never be shared publicly or with the accused party without explicit authorization.
4. **Response Timeline:** The moderation team will review the incident, investigate facts, and provide a formal response within **48 hours**.

---

## 6. Conflict of Interest

If a moderation team member or maintainer is directly involved in an incident or complaint, they are recused from the investigation and decision-making process. The remaining maintainers will adjudicate the matter impartially.
